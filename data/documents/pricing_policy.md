# Pricing Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how product prices, discounts, and pricing-related customer requests should be handled.

## Product Pricing
The current product price shown to a customer is the authoritative selling price for that transaction unless an approved pricing exception applies.

Historical transaction analysis should use the transaction-level price recorded for the order rather than the current catalog price.

## Discounts
Discounts may only be applied when they are supported by an active promotion or an approved business exception.

Support agents must not manually promise an unapproved discount.

## Pricing Errors
If a customer reports a pricing discrepancy:
1. Verify the order and transaction details.
2. Compare the charged amount with the applicable transaction price.
3. Determine whether the discrepancy resulted from a promotion, data issue, or customer misunderstanding.
4. Escalate unresolved material discrepancies.

## Analytics Guidance
For revenue analysis, use transaction-level order item prices. Do not assume that the current product catalog price represents the historical amount paid.

For order-level metrics, avoid summing transaction values across a denormalized one-to-many view without checking for duplicate order rows.

## Exceptions
Material pricing disputes, repeated pricing defects, or cases involving regulatory or contractual concerns should be escalated to the appropriate business owner.
