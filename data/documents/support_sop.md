# Customer Support SOP

## Purpose
This SOP defines the standard workflow for handling customer business questions.

## Standard Workflow
1. Identify the customer's question.
2. Verify the relevant order, product, or account information.
3. Determine whether the question is policy-based, data-based, or requires both.
4. Retrieve the applicable policy guidance.
5. Check factual business data when required.
6. Provide a concise answer supported by the available evidence.
7. Escalate when the available information is insufficient or conflicting.

## Policy Questions
Policy questions should be answered using the approved business knowledge base.

Do not invent policy terms when the relevant policy cannot be found.

## Data Questions
Data questions should be answered using validated business database queries.

Generated SQL must be read-only and must not modify the database.

## Combined Questions
Questions requiring both policy interpretation and business data should use both sources before making a recommendation.

## Customer Communication
Support agents should clearly distinguish:
- Confirmed facts
- Policy requirements
- Reasonable recommendations
- Information that still requires verification

Never promise an exception without authorization.

## Escalation
Escalate cases involving fraud indicators, legal threats, major financial impact, repeated complaints, conflicting policies, or insufficient evidence.
