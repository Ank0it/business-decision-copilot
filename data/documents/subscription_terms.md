# Subscription Terms

## Purpose
This document defines general rules for subscription-related customer support.

## Subscription Changes
Customers may request cancellation or changes to an active subscription according to the terms presented when the subscription was purchased.

Support agents must verify the customer's subscription status before confirming any change.

## Cancellation
A cancellation request should be recorded with:
- Customer identity
- Subscription identifier
- Request date
- Requested effective date
- Reason when provided

Cancellation does not automatically imply a refund. Refund eligibility must be evaluated separately under the refund policy and the terms applicable to the subscription.

## Billing
Billing questions should be checked against the customer's actual transaction history. Agents should not infer a charge solely from a subscription status.

## Exceptions
Requests involving disputed charges, repeated billing failures, suspected account misuse, or contractual disputes should be escalated.

## Business Guidance
Subscription status, cancellation, and refund decisions are separate concepts. A cancelled subscription should not automatically be interpreted as a refunded transaction.
