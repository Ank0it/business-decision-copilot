# Escalation Matrix

## Purpose
This matrix defines when a customer or business issue should be escalated instead of resolved directly by standard support workflows.

## Immediate Escalation
Escalate immediately when a case involves:
- Suspected fraud or systematic abuse
- Legal threats or regulatory complaints
- Serious security concerns
- A material financial exposure
- Conflicting instructions from different business policies

## Financial Escalation
Escalate when:
- A requested exception has material financial impact.
- A payment or transaction discrepancy cannot be reconciled.
- A customer repeatedly requests high-value refunds.
- A pricing error appears to affect multiple customers.

## Operational Escalation
Escalate repeated delivery failures, widespread product issues, or evidence of a systemic operational problem.

## Data Escalation
Escalate when:
- Required business data is unavailable.
- Database results conflict with the source evidence.
- A question requires information that is not represented in the approved schema.
- The requested analysis would require inventing data or unsupported fields.

## Policy Escalation
Escalate when:
- The relevant policy is missing.
- Two policies appear to conflict.
- An exception is requested that is not covered by policy.
- The support agent cannot determine eligibility confidently.

## Decision Guidance
When escalation is required, record:
- Customer or order reference
- Issue summary
- Relevant evidence
- Policy considered
- Data considered
- Reason for escalation
- Recommended next owner

Do not make unsupported commitments while an escalated case is under review.
