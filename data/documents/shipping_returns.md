# Shipping and Returns Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines operational guidance for shipping, delivery, and product returns.

## Shipping
Customers should receive accurate information about the order's shipping and delivery status.

For analytics, distinguish between:
- Order purchase time
- Carrier handoff
- Customer delivery
- Estimated delivery

Do not treat an estimated delivery date as proof that an order was actually delivered.

## Delivery Issues
Late, missing, or damaged deliveries should be investigated using the order's recorded status and delivery timestamps.

When the customer reports a delivery problem, verify the order before offering compensation or a refund.

## Returns
A return may be required before a refund is completed when the applicable policy requires physical inspection of the item.

The return process should record:
- Order
- Item
- Return reason
- Customer instructions
- Relevant timestamps

## Business Guidance
Do not infer that every cancelled or undelivered order is automatically eligible for a refund. Apply the refund policy together with the actual order status and circumstances.

## Escalation
Escalate repeated delivery failures, suspected carrier issues, high-value shipments, and cases where delivery records conflict with the customer's report.
