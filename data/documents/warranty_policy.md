# Warranty Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines general guidance for warranty-related customer requests.

## Warranty Eligibility
A warranty request should be evaluated using:
- Product information
- Purchase or order information
- Reported defect
- Relevant dates
- Applicable warranty terms

Warranty coverage generally concerns product defects and does not automatically cover damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, or unauthorized modification.

## Warranty vs Refund
Warranty support and refund eligibility are separate decisions.

A customer may qualify for warranty assistance without automatically qualifying for a refund.

Refund requests must be evaluated under the refund policy.

## Verification
Support should verify the relevant order and product before confirming warranty coverage.

When evidence is required, request the appropriate information before making a final determination.

## Exceptions
Unusual warranty claims, repeated claims for the same product, suspected abuse, or high-value exceptions should be escalated.

## Business Guidance
Do not state that a product is covered solely because the customer reports a defect. Coverage must be checked against the applicable policy and available evidence.
