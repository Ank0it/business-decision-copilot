# Refund Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines when customers may request a refund and how refund requests should be handled.

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund when:
- The order was cancelled before fulfillment.
- The delivered item is materially defective or damaged.
- The customer received an incorrect item.
- The item is eligible for return under the applicable return conditions.

Refund eligibility is determined from the order status, delivery information, reason for the request, and applicable return or warranty rules.

## Non-Eligible Situations
A refund should normally not be approved when:
- The customer is requesting a refund outside the applicable return period.
- The product was damaged through customer misuse.
- The item is explicitly marked as non-returnable under the applicable product terms.
- Required return evidence or documentation is missing when such evidence is required.

## Refund Process
1. Verify the order and customer information.
2. Determine whether the request satisfies the applicable eligibility conditions.
3. If a return is required, provide return instructions.
4. Inspect or verify the returned item when applicable.
5. Approve or reject the refund with a documented reason.
6. Once approved, process the refund through the original payment method where possible.

## Business Guidance
Support agents must not promise a refund before eligibility has been verified. When policy interpretation is uncertain, escalate the case rather than making an unsupported commitment.

## Escalation
Escalate cases involving suspected fraud, repeated refund abuse, high-value exceptions, legal threats, or conflicting policy requirements.
